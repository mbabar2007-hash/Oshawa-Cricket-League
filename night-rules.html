Night League Rules

Modifications from the Laws of Cricket

  • Each team bats for a set period of time: 55 minutes or 15 overs.
  • Bowler bowls from run-up no longer than 15 yards.
  • Each bowler will be limited to 3 overs.
  • Ball: PVC Soft Plastic Ball (with seam).
  • Fielding Restrictions: First 4 overs - Powerplay. Only 2 fielders allowed outside the Fielding Restriction Area. Remaining overs - 4 fielders must stay within the restriction area. At least 4 fielders on each side.
  • WIDE and NO BALL: ICC Standard Applies.
  • WIDE delivery: 1 run (plus any additional runs) for the batting side. Batter can be OUT – stumped.
  • Persistent wides may lead to a 5 RUN penalty.
  • NO BALL: Free hit on the next ball. Batters can score runs without being OUT (except run out).
  • Innings Last Over: Umpire declares last over with 4 minutes left.
  • BYES: Runs taken because the ball is missed by wicketkeeper.
  • LEG BYES: Runs scored if ball hits legs after batter's shot.
  • Batters are OUT when bowled, caught, run out, stumped, or hit wicket. LBW is not enforced.

Tournament Specific Rules

The Team

  • Minimum 9 players per team; remaining 2-3 players follow guidelines.
  • Fielding: Maximum 10 players on the field. 4 fielders per side. Rotation required for extras.
  • Batting: Maximum 11 players; 10 wickets per team.
  • Substitutes allowed if team has <10 players.
  • Fair Participation Rule: Bowler must bowl 2 overs, batter must face 6 balls min. Batter must score 10 runs or face 12 balls before retiring.
  • Open to everyone aged 16+.
  • Teams may be responsible for scoring matches using CricClubs App.

Equipment

  • Wickets: Kwik-Cricket Plastic Wickets + Metal Stumps.
  • Ball: Black Ash PVC Ball.
  • Bats: Bring your own (no fiber or tape ball bats).
  • Batting Pads and Gloves: optional.
  • Wicketkeeping Gloves: Provided by teams.

Team Uniforms

  • OCL Coloured Jersey and Black Pants/Shorts required. 5 RUN penalty for violations.
  • Extra jerseys should be kept for substitutes.

Game Times

  • Match duration: 115 minutes.
  • Coin toss 5 minutes before scheduled start. Minimum 6 players in uniform required.
  • Delays: 5 RUN penalty for every 2 minutes late beyond 1 minute.
  • Overs: 12 overs by 45-minute mark. Maximum 15 overs per innings.
  • Slow over rates: 2-point penalty.

Ground Rules

  • Civic Stadium: Field divided into 2 playing areas separated by the 55-yard line.
  • Boundary Rules: 4 runs if ball rolls out; 6 runs if ball flies out.
  • Extra balls provided if ball hit over boundary.
  • Ball hitting football goal post: Play is DEAD, 1 run plus extras.

Season & Playoffs

  • Points: Win 4 pts, Tie/No Result 2 pts, Penalty -2 pts.
  • Standings tie-breaker: 1. Net Run Rate 2. Coin Flip
  • Playoff format depends on number of teams.
  • Knockout Match Tiebreaker: Fewest Wickets Lost → Fewest Balls Batted → Super Over.
  • Playoffs Coin Toss before each match.

Captain's Responsibilities

  • Set up field before match. Pick up cones, wickets, and balls.
  • Maintain player respect for umpires and opponents.
  • Ensure fair participation; infractions = 2-point penalty.
  • Team scorer must know CricClubs app.

Scoring App - CricClubs

  • 2-3 players per team must have the app approved for scoring.
  • Disable Pitch Map and Directions.
  • Record WIDE, NO BALL, BYE, LEG BYE, and WICKETS carefully.
  • Retired batters can be restored as per rules.

Remember

  • Batters switch ends at the end of overs.
  • New batter on strike after wicket unless over ended.
  • Umpire makes final decisions on NO BALL and WIDE.
  • Fielding honor system for boundaries and catches.
  • No alcohol allowed on the facility.
  • All players must register and agree to Waiver Form.
  • Players under 18 require guardian signature.
