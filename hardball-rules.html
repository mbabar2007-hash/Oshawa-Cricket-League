Outdoor Hardball League Rules

Register Now

Modifications from the Laws of Cricket

  • Each team bats for a set period of time: 80 minutes or 20 overs.
  • Each bowler will be limited to 4 overs.
  • Ball: Hard Leather Ball (White - Black Ash Sports).
  • Fielding Restrictions: First 6 overs - Powerplay. Only 2 fielders outside the 30-yard Fielding Restriction Area. Remaining overs - 4 fielders must stay within the restriction area. See Diagram Below.
  • WIDE and NO BALL: ICC Standard Applies.
  • WIDE delivery: 1 run (+ additional runs) for batting side; batter can be OUT - stumped.
  • NO BALL: Next ball is a FREE HIT. Batter can score runs without risk of being OUT (except run out). After 3rd WIDE/NO BALL, subsequent deliveries = 2 runs (+ any additional runs).
  • Innings Last Over: Umpire declares last over with 4 minutes left. All WIDE balls rebowled.
  • BYES: Runs taken because the ball is missed by wicketkeeper.
  • LEG BYES: Runs scored if ball hits legs after batter's shot.
  • Batters are OUT when bowled, caught, run out, stumped, or hit wicket.
  • LBW will be enforced fully or on deliberate blocking of the wickets.

Tournament Specific Rules

The Team

  • Minimum 9 players per team; remaining players follow guidelines.
  • Fielding: Maximum 11 players on the field.
  • Batting: Maximum 11 players; 10 wickets per team.
  • Substitutes allowed if team has <11 players.
  • Fair Participation: Bowler must bowl 2 overs, batter must face 6 balls min. Batter must score 10 runs or face 12 balls before retiring.
  • Open to everyone aged 16+.
  • Teams may be responsible for scoring matches using CricClubs App.

Equipment

  • Ball: Hard Leather Ball (provided).
  • Stumps and Field Markers: Provided.
  • Bats, Batting Pads, Guards, Helmets, Wicketkeeping Pads & Gloves: Must be provided by each team.
  • Abdominal Guard must be worn by individual players.

Team Uniforms

  • OCL Logo Shirt and Black Pants (no shorts). 5 RUN penalty for violations.
  • Extra uniforms required for substitutes.

Game Times

  • Match duration: 170 minutes.
  • Coin toss 10 minutes before start. Minimum 6 players in uniform required.
  • Delays: 5 RUN penalty every 2 minutes after 1 minute delay.
  • Overs: 18 overs by 70-minute mark; 20 overs max per innings.
  • 10-minute break between innings. DLS applied if 2nd team cannot complete overs.

Ground Rules

  • Stone Street Park: Batting only on South Side of the Wicket.
  • Playing area marked by paint or pylons.
  • 4 runs for ball rolling out; 6 runs for ball flying out.

Season & Playoffs

  • Points: Win 4 pts, Tie/No Result 2 pts, Penalty -2 pts.
  • Standings tie-breaker: 1. Net Run Rate 2. Coin Flip
  • Playoff format depends on number of teams.
  • Knockout Match Tiebreaker: Fewest Wickets Lost → Fewest Balls Batted → Super Over.
  • Playoff rosters: Player must play in at least half of regular season matches.

Captain's Responsibilities

  • Set up field before match. Pick up markers, bats, wickets, balls.
  • Maintain respect for umpires and opponents.
  • Ensure fair participation; infractions = 2-point penalty.
  • Team scorer must know CricClubs app.

Scoring App - CricClubs

  • 2-3 players per team must have the app approved for scoring.
  • Disable Pitch Map and Directions.
  • Record WIDE, NO BALL, BYE, LEG BYE, WICKETS carefully.
  • Retired batters can be restored as per rules.

Remember

  • Batters switch ends at end of overs.
  • Retired batters can return only when all other batters are OUT.
  • Umpire makes final decisions on NO BALL and WIDE.
  • Fielding honor system for boundaries and catches.
  • No alcohol allowed on the facility.
  • All players must register and agree to Waiver Form.
  • Players under 18 require guardian signature.
