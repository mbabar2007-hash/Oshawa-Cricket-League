Youth Hard Leather Ball Rules

Modifications from the Laws of Cricket

  • Each team bats for 55 minutes or 15 overs
  • Each bowler limited to 3 overs
  • Ball: Hard Leather Ball (White – Black Ash Sports)
  • Fielding Restrictions: First 4 overs – Powerplay (2 fielders outside 30-yard area), remaining overs 4 fielders within restriction area
  • WIDE & NO BALL: ICC Standard Applies
  • WIDE: Any ball outside marker or behind batter = 1 run (+additional runs). Batter can be OUT - Stumped. After 2nd wide in over, subsequent wides = 2 runs
  • NO BALL: Overstep, throw, chuck, or waist-high ball. Next ball is FREE HIT. 1 run penalty applied
  • Innings Last Over: Umpire declares last over with 4 minutes remaining; all WIDE balls rebowled
  • BYES: Runs scored because ball missed by wicketkeeper
  • LEG BYES: Runs scored when ball hits legs after batter’s shot
  • Batters OUT when bowled, caught, run out, stumped, or hit wicket
  • LBW enforced fully, especially on deliberate wicket blocking

Tournament Specific Rules

  • Teams: Minimum 9 players; maximum 11 on field
  • Batting: Maximum 11 players; 10 wickets per team
  • Substitutes allowed if team < 11 players
  • Fair Participation: Bowler must bowl 2 overs, batter must face 6 balls. Batter must score 10 runs or face 12 balls before retiring. Retirements only at over end or fall of wicket
  • Open to all aged 14+ (or younger with parent permission)
  • Teams may be responsible for scoring via CricClubs App

Equipment

  • Ball: Hard Leather Ball (provided)
  • Stumps & Field Markers: Provided
  • Bats, Pads, Guards, Helmets, Wicketkeeping Gloves: Provided. Abdominal guard must be worn

Team Uniforms

  • OCL Jersey & Black Pants (no shorts) required
  • 5 RUN penalty for violations; substitutes exempt if extra jersey provided

Game Times

  • Match duration: 120 minutes
  • Coin toss 10 minutes prior; 6 players in uniform required
  • Delays: 5 RUN penalty every 2 minutes after 1-minute delay
  • Overs: 12 overs by 50-minute mark; first innings ends at 55 min
  • 10-minute break; second team bats same number of overs (max 15)
  • DLS applied if second team cannot complete overs

Ground Rules

  • DCDSB Field: Batting on North Side of Wicket only
  • Playing area marked by paint/pylons
  • 4 runs for ball rolling out; 6 runs for ball flying out

Season & Playoffs

  • Points: Win 4, Tie/No Result 2, Penalty -2
  • Tie-breaker: 1. Net Run Rate 2. Coin Flip
  • Knockout Match Tiebreaker: Fewest Wickets Lost → Fewest Balls Batted → Super Over
  • Playoff Rosters: Players must play at least half of regular season matches

Captain's Responsibilities

  • Set up field, pick up markers, bats, wickets, balls
  • Maintain respect & ensure fair participation (2-point penalty for infractions)
  • Team scorer must know CricClubs app

Scoring App - CricClubs

  • 2-3 players per team must be approved to score
  • Disable Pitch Map & Directions
  • Record WIDE, NO BALL, BYE, LEG BYE, WICKETS accurately
  • Retired batters restored only when allowed
  • Undo errors with ↩️ button

Remember

  • Batters switch ends at the end of overs
  • Retired batters return only when all others are OUT
  • Umpire makes final decisions on NO BALL & WIDE
  • Fielding honor system for boundaries & catches
  • No alcohol allowed
  • Players must register & agree to waiver form
  • Players under 18 require guardian signature
