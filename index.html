About Us

The Oshawa Cricket League, is a community-focused organization dedicated to growing and promoting cricket in Oshawa and the Durham Region. We provide a well-organized, competitive, and inclusive platform for players of all skill levels while upholding the values of sportsmanship, respect, and fair play.

Our Mission is to develop and promote cricket by offering a structured, competitive, and welcoming league that supports player growth, teamwork, and community engagement.

Our Vision is to become a leading community cricket league that inspires participation, nurtures talent, and unites people through a shared passion for the game.
