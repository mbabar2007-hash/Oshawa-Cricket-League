Indoor League Rules

Register Now

Modifications from the Laws of Cricket

  • Each team bats for a set period of time (36 minutes) or 12 overs.
  • Bowler bowls from a run-up no longer than 10 yards.
  • Each bowler is limited to 2 overs.
  • Ball: PVC Soft Plastic Ball.
  • Fielding Restrictions:
    • First 4 overs - Powerplay: Only 2 fielders allowed outside the fielding restriction area.
    • Remaining overs: 4 fielders (including 1 slip fielder) must be within the fielding restriction area.
    • Minimum 4 fielders on each side of the field. If 10 fielders, 1 must stand in slip.
  • Wide and No Ball Rules:
    • First two in the over will be re-bowled. Thereafter, 2 RUNs awarded (plus any additional runs completed by batters) and ball not re-bowled.
    • All wides and no balls will be re-bowled in the last over of each innings.
    • Wide delivery: Any ball passing outside the wide marker or behind the batter. Batter can be stumped out on a wide ball.
    • Persistent wides may lead to a 5 RUN penalty for unfair play.
    • No Ball: Bowler oversteps, throws, or bowls over batter’s waist. Next ball is a FREE HIT; runs scored allowed without dismissal (except run out).
  • Innings Last Over: Umpire declares last over with 4 minutes left. No new over to be bowled after 35 minutes.
  • Byes: Runs taken when ball is missed by wicketkeeper.
  • Leg Byes: Runs taken when ball hits batter’s legs after missing the shot.
  • The batter is OUT when bowled, caught, run out, stumped, hits roof, or hits wicket.
  • Leg before wicket (LBW) not enforced. Fourth stump added. Batter moving across to block stumps may affect umpire WIDE decision.

Tournament Specific Rules

The Team

  • Minimum 8 players per team. Remaining players follow guidelines below.
  • Fielding: Maximum 10 players on field (1 slip required at all times). If 11 players, 1 may sit out for 2 overs.
  • Batting: Maximum 11 players can bat; 10 wickets per team.
  • If team has fewer than 10 players, a player may be borrowed from substitute list or inactive team.
  • Fair Participation Rule: Each player must bat/bowl minimum. Automatic loss and 2-point penalty if not met. Minimums: Bowler 2 overs, batter 6 balls. Batter must score 10 runs or face 8 balls before retiring.
  • Open to everyone aged 16+.
  • Teams may be responsible for scoring matches via CricClubs app.

The Equipment

  • Wickets: Metal or Kwik-Cricket plastic stumps.
  • Ball: PVC Cricket Ball.
  • Bats: Bring your own. Tape Ball Bats and Fiber Bats are not permitted.
  • Batting pads and gloves: Not required.
  • Wicketkeeping gloves must be provided by the teams.

Team Uniforms

  • OCL shirts and black pants/shorts must be worn.
  • 5 RUN penalty for anyone batting or fielding without proper uniform.
  • Teams should order extra shirts for substitutes.

Game Times

  • Matches scheduled for 75 minutes.
  • Coin toss: 5 minutes before start. Minimum 6 players in uniform required.
  • First innings ends at 36 minutes; second innings bats same number of overs (max 12).
  • Team batting second continues until losing all wickets or time runs out.

Ground Rules

  • Civic Fieldhouse: Soccer field divided into 2 playing areas with partition wall.
  • Boundaries: 4 runs if ball rolls out; 6 runs if ball flies out.
  • Balls hitting side walls or screen: Caught - OUT if fielder in play area.
  • Balls hitting ceiling/roof: Considered OUT (FREE HIT exception applies).

Season and Playoffs

  • Points: Win = 4, Tie/No Result = 2, Penalty = -2.
  • Standings tie-breaker: 1. Net Run Rate 2. Coin Flip.
  • Playoff format determined by number of teams.
  • Playoff rosters: Restrictions may apply to substitutes (must be registered).
  • Knockout tiebreaker: 1. Fewest wickets lost 2. Fewest balls batted 3. Super Over.

Captain's Responsibilities

  • League organizers set field before each match.
  • Home team captain picks up markers, wickets, balls, and equipment.
  • Maintain over rates and respect for umpires and opponents. 2-point penalty for infractions.
  • Do not engage in prolonged discussions with umpires or fielders regarding decisions.
  • Ensure team scorer is familiar with the CricClubs scoring app.

Scoring App - CricClubs

  • 2–3 players per team must have the app and be approved for scoring.
  • Before scoring begins, click on Actions: Disable Pitch Map & Directions.
  • If BYE or LEG BYE scored: select Extras and enter appropriate amount.
  • If a WICKET falls: select Wicket, record player details (catches, stumpings, run outs).
  • If a batter retires: use Retire Batsman option in Actions.
  • Errors can be undone with ↩️ undo button.
  • Batters switch ends after each over. Retired batters return only when others are out.
  • Umpire: OCL provides main umpire; batting side may provide leg umpire.
  • Fielding: Honour system for boundaries, catches, and outs.
  • Respect: Abuse of staff, competitors, umpires, or officials will not be tolerated.
  • Alcohol: Not allowed anywhere on the facility.
  • Registration & Waivers: Players must register and agree to waiver before participating.
  • Underage players, under 18, require parent/guardian waiver signature.
